Privacy Policy – Android

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Effective: November 10, 2026

AI Girl is operated by SSW AS, a Norwegian company. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use our Android application (distributed through Google Play), how we use it, and your rights as a user. By using the app you agree to the practices described here. The iOS app has its own policy at sswinvest.no/aigirl/privacy.

Age requirement: AI Girl is strictly intended for users aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will delete their data immediately.

1. Data Controller

SSW AS
Norway
Email: post@sswinvest.no

2. Information We Collect

Automatically collected:

  • Device identifier (Android ID) — used solely to link your characters to your device and to apply fair-use limits. It is never tied to your name or Google account and cannot be used to identify you across other apps.
  • Anonymous usage statistics — how many messages are sent (never what they say), which app photos are shown, when the subscription screen is shown, when a new character is created, and when a subscription starts or ends, together with the app version, the Android version and the country of your Google Play account. These are linked only to a random ID created on your device (not your device identifier), and never include your messages.

Information you provide:

  • Character preferences created during onboarding (name, age, appearance, personality traits, language preference)
  • Messages you send in chat
  • Reports — if you press and hold an AI reply or photo to report it, we receive the reported reply or photo and the reason you chose. Your own messages are not included.

We do NOT collect:

  • Your name, email address, or Google account
  • Location data
  • Contacts, or photos and files from your device
  • Audio — the Android app has no voice input and never uses the microphone
  • Health or financial information
  • Data from other apps or websites

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and display your AI companion characters
  • To store your characters (your chat messages stay on your device)
  • To personalise AI responses based on information you share in chat
  • To generate AI images based on character settings you chose
  • To show notifications from your companion (only if you allow them)
  • To review reported content and improve safety
  • To maintain the service, prevent misuse, and improve reliability

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — given on the consent screen when you first open the app. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your character in the app or deleting the app.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — processing necessary to deliver the service you have subscribed to.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — basic app functionality, security, and fraud prevention.

5. Tracking and Advertising

AI Girl does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not:

  • Use the Android advertising ID
  • Show ads or build advertising profiles
  • Share data with data brokers or ad networks
  • Use third-party analytics SDKs that profile behaviour

7. Consent for AI Data Processing

By using AI Girl you explicitly consent to the following data being sent to third-party AI providers in order to deliver core app functionality:

  • Anthropic (Claude): your chat messages and character settings are sent to Anthropic's API to generate AI responses. Anthropic is based in the United States.
  • Fal.ai: your character's appearance settings are sent to Fal.ai's API to generate AI images. Fal.ai is based in the United States.

Without this, the core chat and image features cannot function. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your character or the app, which stops all data transmission to these providers.

8. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers are based in the United States. When your data is processed by these providers it is transferred outside the EU/EEA. We ensure adequate protection through Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as approved by the European Commission.

9. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

Your character profiles are stored on Supabase servers (EU region) and on your device. Your chat messages and the photos you receive are stored only on your device; uninstalling the app deletes them. Data is associated only with your device ID or a random ID — never with your name, email, or Google account.

How to delete your data:

  • In the app: open the chat, tap the gear icon → Delete girlfriend and start over. This permanently deletes your character from our servers and all chat messages and photos on your phone.
  • By email: write to post@sswinvest.no to have all data linked to your device deleted, including anonymous statistics and reports. We delete it within 30 days of your request.

Otherwise we keep your data for as long as you use the app.

10. In-App Purchases

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Google Play. Payment is processed entirely by Google; we receive only the purchase status (whether your subscription is active) and never see your payment details.

11. Notifications

Notifications are optional and requested during onboarding (on Android 13 and later through the system permission dialog). They are scheduled on your device; we do not store a notification token. You can turn them off at any time in the app settings or in Android Settings.

12. Reporting AI Content

You can report any AI reply or photo by pressing and holding it in the chat and choosing a reason. Reports are used only to review the content and improve safety.

13. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. The app contains no secret keys; all AI calls go through our protected servers.

14. Children's Privacy

AI Girl is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used this app, please contact us immediately at post@sswinvest.no and we will delete all associated data.

15. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR and the Norwegian Personal Data Act (personopplysningsloven) you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, data portability, objection, restriction of processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at post@sswinvest.no. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with Datatilsynet (the Norwegian Data Protection Authority) at datatilsynet.no.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
